## Choose by constraint, not by hype

Most guides tell you which tool to use. This book asks what you are building, for whom, and under which limits, then walks through the options that fit and the price of each one.

## About this project

This is a living, community-reviewed reference. The first draft was written with the help of AI tools and is being reviewed, sourced and extended chapter by chapter, in the open. Each page shows its review status: drafts are clearly marked until they pass the [content standards](https://github.com/blip-cmd/frontend-engineering-book-of-choices/blob/main/CONTENT_STANDARDS.md), and reviewed pages show who checked them and when. If you spot a mistake, please [open an issue](https://github.com/blip-cmd/frontend-engineering-book-of-choices/issues).

The writing is free to read and share under [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://github.com/blip-cmd/frontend-engineering-book-of-choices/blob/main/LICENSE-CONTENT.md).
